DRIFTAE

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Driftae sells diecast model cars to collectors in New Zealand. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and how you can see, correct or delete it. We follow the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

What we collect

  • When you buy from us (on Trade Me, through our website or by message): your name, delivery address, phone or email, what you bought, the price paid and the shipment tracking number.
  • When you message us on Facebook, Instagram or Messenger: your name, profile name and the content of the conversation.
  • When you visit driftae.com: standard technical information such as browser type, pages viewed and how you arrived. If we use advertising tools such as the Meta Pixel, they may record visits and actions on the site so we can measure our ads.

We do not collect payment card details; payments are handled by Trade Me, banks or other payment providers.

How we use it

  • To process, ship and support your order, and to answer your questions.
  • To keep business and tax records.
  • To understand which cars and posts our customers like, so we stock and promote the right models. We look at this in summary form (for example "1:18 rally cars sell well"), not to build profiles of individuals.
  • To show and measure our ads on Facebook and Instagram.

We do not sell your personal information.

Our email list

If you ask to join (by ticking the box on our website, or by replying with your email when we ask in Messenger), we email you new arrivals and members-only deals. We keep your email address, when and how you said yes, and nothing else for this list. Every email has an unsubscribe link; once you use it, or tell us to stop, you won't get offers from us again. We never add Trade Me buyers or anyone else to the list without asking, and we never share or sell it.

Who we share it with

  • Couriers and NZ Post, to deliver your order.
  • Trade Me, Meta (Facebook, Instagram, Messenger) and payment providers, when you use their services to deal with us.
  • Service providers that host our order records and tools. Some of them store data outside New Zealand; we choose providers that protect it to a standard comparable to New Zealand law.
  • Anyone we are required to share it with by law.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for seven years because New Zealand tax law requires it. We keep messages only as long as we need them to help you.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct it. You can also ask us to delete it, unless we have to keep it by law (for example, order records for tax).

Deleting your data

To ask us to delete your information, including data received through our Facebook or Instagram apps, send us a message on our Facebook Page with the subject "Delete my data" and the name or profile you used with us. We will confirm and complete the request within 20 working days.

Contact and complaints

For any privacy question, message us on our Facebook Page. If you are not happy with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change. The date at the top shows the latest version.
